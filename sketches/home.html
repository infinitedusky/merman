One scene, three ways to look at it

The camera animates between views, so you keep your place.

[ flat ]

Flat

It reads like the Mermaid you know, for docs and screenshots.

[ iso ]

Isometric

Subgraphs become trays and nodes have depth, so you see the structure at a glance.

[ 3d ]

3D

Orbit, zoom and fly into a subgraph to walk through a big system.

Big diagrams stop being a wall of boxes

Nest subgraphs as deep as you like. Click a tray to fly in, or drag a node and its edges re-route live.

Status: prototype

Early, and honest about it

It works on the samples. There are rough edges on large diagrams and no tests yet. If you want to help, these are good first issues:

  • Edges that cross subgraphs on big diagrams
  • Readable edge labels at every zoom
  • True orthographic camera for flat / iso
  • More Mermaid shapes, classDef / style
  • Export to PNG / GLB
  • See the full list in CONTRIBUTING →
